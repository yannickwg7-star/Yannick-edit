# REBORN: retention-first motion piece (1080×1920, 60fps, 45.7s)

The voiceover is the user's reference speech: Isagi Yoichi's "reborn" speech from Blue Lock, stitched from five screen recordings. The audio is not committed. Everything visual is generated by `reborn.js`.

## Retention architecture
- **Central focal zone.** Objects sit at (540, 740) and the type band at y≈1390. Nothing important sits near the edges.
- **The anchor.** One glowing focus ring stays at centre for the whole video and becomes each object in turn: clock, door frame, mirror, the rebirth counter.
- **Pattern interrupts.** One fires every 2.208s (6 beats at 163 BPM), cycling through glow shift, depth-of-field pulse, 3D tilt (camera roll plus yaw of the wireframe floor) and grid warp.
- **Camera.** It pushes in slowly toward every drop (sine ease, +5.5%), then snaps back (+9% kick, exp decay) with a micro-shake and a 0.18s flash on the drop.
- **Typography.** Words pop one by one on an analytic damped spring (3.4Hz, ζ=0.38). The active word gets a bloom. Earlier words dim to 38% monochrome. Accent words glow amber `#FFAB00` or emerald `#00E676`.
- **Background.** Slate `#0B0D11`, a scrolling 3D wireframe floor with a wave mesh, 110 depth-of-field particles (6 blur levels with a drifting focus plane), a vignette and film grain.

## Build
```
node render.cjs frames                      # 2742 JPEG frames, 2-sample motion blur
ffmpeg -framerate 60 -i frames/f%04d.jpg -i speech.wav -c:v libx264 -crf 23 -pix_fmt yuv420p -c:a aac -shortest reborn.mp4
```
Open `index.html` for a live preview.
